## Formal definition

TD3 extends DDPG with three critical improvements for stable continuous control: (1) Clipped double-Q: $y = r + \gamma \min_{i=1,2} Q_{\bar{\theta}_i}(s', \tilde{a}')$ using two critics to combat overestimation; (2) Delayed policy updates: update the actor every $d = 2$ critic updates to let the critics stabilize; (3) Target policy smoothing: $\tilde{a}' = \pi_{\bar{\phi}}(s') + \text{clip}(\epsilon, -c, c), \; \epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$ adding clipped noise to the target action, which smooths the $Q$-function and prevents exploitation of narrow $Q$-peaks. The actor is a deterministic policy $a = \pi_\phi(s)$ updated by $\nabla_\phi J = \mathbb{E}[\nabla_a Q_{\theta_1}(s,a)|_{a=\pi_\phi(s)} \nabla_\phi \pi_\phi(s)]$.

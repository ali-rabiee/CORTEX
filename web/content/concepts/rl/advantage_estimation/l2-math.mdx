## Formal definition

GAE provides a family of advantage estimators parameterized by $\lambda \in [0,1]$ that interpolate between high-bias/low-variance (TD residual, $\lambda=0$) and low-bias/high-variance (Monte Carlo, $\lambda=1$): $$\hat{A}_t^{\text{GAE}(\gamma,\lambda)} = \sum_{l=0}^{\infty} (\gamma\lambda)^l \delta_{t+l}, \quad \text{where} \quad \delta_t = r_t + \gamma V_\phi(s_{t+1}) - V_\phi(s_t)$$ This is the exponentially-weighted average of $n$-step advantage estimators $\hat{A}_t^{(n)}$. The bias comes from the value function approximation error $V_\phi \neq V^\pi$; the variance from summing stochastic rewards over long horizons.

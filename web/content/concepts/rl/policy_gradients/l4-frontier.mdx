## Three eras, one estimator

**Era 1 — make it work (1992–2016).** REINFORCE established the estimator;
A3C/A2C tamed its variance with learned baselines and parallel rollouts.

**Era 2 — make it scale (2017–2020).** PPO made each batch reusable; IMPALA
made collection distributed. Together they fixed the two bottlenecks
(stability, throughput) that kept PGs out of serious applications.

**Era 3 — change the substrate (2022–now).** The biggest policy-gradient
deployments today optimize *language models*: RLHF with PPO, then
critic-free group-relative variants (GRPO) for reasoning. In robotics, PGs
power massively-parallel sim training; in the VLA era they're increasingly
the *fine-tuning* stage on top of imitation-pretrained policies.

Interview-ready synthesis: "Policy gradients never got a fundamentally new
estimator after 1992 — the field's progress is a 30-year campaign of
variance reduction (baselines, GAE), trust management (TRPO/PPO), and
systems engineering (A3C, IMPALA). The latest twist, GRPO, even *removes*
the critic — proof that knowing the variance/bias levers matters more than
memorizing any single algorithm."

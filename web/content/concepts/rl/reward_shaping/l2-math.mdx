## Formal definition

Reward shaping modifies the reward function to provide denser learning signals while preserving the optimal policy. Potential-based shaping is defined as: $$R'(s, a, s') = R(s, a, s') + \gamma\, \Phi(s') - \Phi(s)$$ where $\Phi: \mathcal{S} \to \mathbb{R}$ is the potential function. Ng et al. (1999) proved that potential-based shaping is the only class that guarantees policy invariance — i.e., $\pi^*_{R'} = \pi^*_R$ for all MDPs.

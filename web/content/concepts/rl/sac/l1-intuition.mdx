## Intuition

SAC is like a policy that's rewarded for being both good and creative. The entropy term $\alpha\, H(\pi)$ encourages exploration by penalizing deterministic behavior. The automatic temperature $\alpha$ finds the right balance — early in training $\alpha$ is high (explore more), later it decreases (exploit more). The dual optimization on $\alpha$ elegantly targets a desired entropy level without manual tuning.

## In practice

Training a dexterous hand to rotate a cube in MuJoCo. SAC reaches 90% success in 2M steps vs PPO needing 10M steps. The replay buffer lets SAC reuse past experience. Key: use two Q-networks with $Q = \min(Q_{\phi_1}, Q_{\phi_2})$ to prevent overestimation. Target network update rate $\tau = 0.005$, learning rate $\alpha_{\text{lr}} = 3 \times 10^{-4}$, replay buffer size $|\mathcal{B}| = 10^6$.

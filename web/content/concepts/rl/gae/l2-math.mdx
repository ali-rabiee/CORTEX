## Formal definition

GAE defines a family of advantage estimators indexed by $\lambda \in [0,1]$: $$\hat{A}_t^{\text{GAE}(\gamma,\lambda)} = \sum_{l=0}^{T-t-1} (\gamma\lambda)^l \delta_{t+l}, \quad \delta_t = r_t + \gamma V_\phi(s_{t+1}) - V_\phi(s_t)$$ This can be shown to equal the exponentially-weighted average of $n$-step advantage estimators: $\hat{A}_t^{\text{GAE}} = (1-\lambda) \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \lambda^{n-1} \hat{A}_t^{(n)}$ where $\hat{A}_t^{(n)} = -V_\phi(s_t) + \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} \gamma^k r_{t+k} + \gamma^n V_\phi(s_{t+n})$. The bias of GAE is $O(\epsilon_{V} / (1 - \gamma\lambda))$ where $\epsilon_V = \max_s |V_\phi(s) - V^\pi(s)|$, and the variance scales as $O(\sigma_r^2 / (1 - \gamma\lambda)^2)$.

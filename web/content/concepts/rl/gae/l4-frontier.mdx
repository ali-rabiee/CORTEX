## Why this 2015 trick refuses to die

GAE solved the right problem at the right altitude: it made the
bias-variance trade-off of multi-step credit assignment a *single scalar*,
orthogonal to the policy optimizer. PPO, IMPALA-style systems, and every
sim-to-real locomotion pipeline since simply inherited it.

The interesting frontier work is not "better GAE" but **what replaces it
when its on-policy assumption breaks**:

Pattern to internalize: **λ-return → (add importance weights) → Retrace →
(clip them for systems reasons) → V-trace.** Each step trades a little bias
for bounded variance under staler data.

<KeyIdea>

Interview-ready synthesis: "GAE is exponential smoothing over TD errors —
two lines of code that subsume n-step returns. It's untouched since 2015
because the empirical studies showed λ≈0.95 is flat-optimal, so the research
moved to its off-policy generalizations (Retrace, V-trace) where the real
open problems were."

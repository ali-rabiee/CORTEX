## Failure modes

Entropy collapse: policy becomes deterministic too early, converging to a single action mode. Symptoms: reward plateaus, action standard deviation $\sigma \to 0$. Root cause: entropy coefficient too low. Fix: increase entropy bonus coefficient to $\beta \in [0.01, 0.05]$, monitor policy entropy $H(\pi)$.

## Interview-ready answer

PPO stabilizes policy gradient training by clipping the importance sampling ratio $r(\theta) = \frac{\pi_{\theta}}{\pi_{\theta_{\text{old}}}}$ between old and new policies. The key insight is the $\min$ of clipped and unclipped objectives — this creates a pessimistic bound that prevents destructive updates. In practice, PPO with $\epsilon=0.2$, batch size 2048, 10 epochs per update, and entropy bonus $\beta=0.01$ is a strong default for continuous control. Compared to TRPO, PPO is simpler (no conjugate gradient, no Fisher vector products) and scales better to large networks.

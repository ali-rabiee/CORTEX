## Formal definition

PPO constrains policy updates by clipping the probability ratio $r(\theta) = \frac{\pi_{\theta}(a|s)}{\pi_{\theta_{\text{old}}}(a|s)}$ to $[1-\epsilon, 1+\epsilon]$, preventing destructively large policy changes. The clipped surrogate objective is: $$L^{\text{CLIP}}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}\left[\min\left(r(\theta)\hat{A}, \;\text{clip}(r(\theta), 1-\epsilon, 1+\epsilon)\hat{A}\right)\right]$$

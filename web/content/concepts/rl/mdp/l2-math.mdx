## Formal definition

An MDP is the formal mathematical framework for sequential decision making, defined by the tuple $(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{A}, P, R, \gamma)$: state space $\mathcal{S}$, action space $\mathcal{A}$, transition dynamics $P(s'|s,a): \mathcal{S} \times \mathcal{A} \times \mathcal{S} \to [0,1]$, reward function $R(s,a,s'): \mathcal{S} \times \mathcal{A} \times \mathcal{S} \to \mathbb{R}$, and discount factor $\gamma \in [0,1)$. The Markov property states that $P(s_{t+1}|s_t, a_t, s_{t-1}, a_{t-1}, \ldots) = P(s_{t+1}|s_t, a_t)$ — the future is conditionally independent of the past given the present state and action.

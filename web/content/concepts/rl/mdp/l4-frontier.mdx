## The formalism is stable; its role is shifting

Nobody has replaced the MDP — every 2024-26 system still bottoms out in
states, actions, and rewards. What changed is **where the MDP comes from**:

1. **Learned, latent MDPs.** When observations are pixels, the winning move
   is to learn a compact Markov state and do RL there.
2. **MDP-free control of MDP problems.** Sequence models solve the decision
   problem with supervised learning, trading Bellman machinery for data
   coverage assumptions.
3. **Generated MDPs.** Foundation world models generate the environment
   itself, promising unlimited interactive training worlds.

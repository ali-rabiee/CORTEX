## Formal definition

Entropy regularization augments the RL objective with a bonus proportional to the policy's entropy, encouraging stochastic behavior: $$J_{\text{ent}}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{\pi_\theta}\left[\sum_{t=0}^{T} \gamma^t \bigl(r_t + \beta\, H(\pi_\theta(\cdot|s_t))\bigr)\right], \quad H(\pi) = -\sum_a \pi(a|s) \log \pi(a|s)$$ For continuous Gaussian policies $\pi = \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2 I)$, the entropy has a closed form: $H = \frac{d}{2}\ln(2\pi e) + \sum_{i=1}^{d} \ln \sigma_i$. SAC takes this further with automatic tuning of $\beta$ (its $\alpha$) to target a desired entropy level $\bar{H}$.

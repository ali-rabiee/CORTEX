## Formal definition

Designing robot learning systems with deployment constraints in mind from day one: inference latency $t_{\text{infer}} < t_{\text{budget}}$, compute budget (FLOPS, memory), failure recovery, monitoring, and graceful degradation. The gap between a research demo and a production robot is mostly systems engineering. Key metrics: latency $p_{99}$, throughput, mean time between failures (MTBF), mean time to recovery (MTTR).

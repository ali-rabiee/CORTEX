## Intuition

A flight simulator for robots. Before letting a robot learn by crashing into things in the real world (expensive, slow, dangerous), you let it crash millions of times in a virtual world. The better the simulator matches reality, the better the trained policy transfers. GPU simulators like Isaac Gym are like running thousands of flight simulators simultaneously.

## In practice

Training a locomotion policy in Isaac Gym: 4096 parallel environments on a single A100 GPU, each simulating a quadruped robot. PPO training converges in ~30 minutes ($\sim$10^9 steps) vs days on CPU-based simulators. Scene setup: randomized terrain (stairs, slopes, rough ground), domain randomization on friction $\mu$, mass $m$, and motor strength. For manipulation: MuJoCo with dm_control for precise contact modeling, or Isaac Sim with NVIDIA Omniverse for photorealistic rendering (needed for vision-based policies).

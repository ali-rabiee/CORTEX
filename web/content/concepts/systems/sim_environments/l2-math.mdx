## Formal definition

Physics simulators used to generate training data and evaluate robot policies before real-world deployment. Major platforms: MuJoCo (fast contact dynamics, $\sim$10M steps/hour on CPU, DeepMind standard), Isaac Gym/Isaac Sim (GPU-accelerated, $\sim$10B steps/hour on A100, NVIDIA ecosystem), PyBullet (open-source, good for prototyping), Drake (focus on contact-rich manipulation, complementarity-based contact). Key properties: physics fidelity, rendering quality, parallelism, API design, and sim2real gap for target domain.

## Formal definition

Continuous verification during robot execution that safety constraints are satisfied. Formalized as monitoring a set of safety predicates $\{\phi_1, \ldots, \phi_M\}$ at each control step, where violation of any predicate triggers a safety response. Common constraints: workspace bounds $\mathbf{p}_{\text{ee}} \in \mathcal{W}_{\text{safe}}$, velocity limits $\|\dot{\mathbf{q}}\| \leq \dot{q}_{\max}$, force/torque limits $\|\boldsymbol{\tau}_{\text{ext}}\| \leq \tau_{\max}$, collision avoidance $d(\text{robot}, \text{obstacle}) \geq d_{\min}$, and self-collision checking. Multi-layered: from low-level hardware e-stops to high-level learned anomaly detection.

## Intuition

A robot reaching for a moving object can't wait 500ms for a diffusion model to generate an action. Latency directly limits control bandwidth — at 100ms latency, you can only correct 10 times per second. Every millisecond matters for reactive, contact-rich tasks.

## In practice

Optimizing a diffusion policy: original (100 denoising steps, FP32) $\rightarrow$ 1.5s per action. Step 1: DDIM ($T=10$ steps) $\rightarrow$ 150ms. Step 2: FP16 inference $\rightarrow$ 100ms. Step 3: TensorRT compilation $\rightarrow$ 60ms. Step 4: async pipeline (predict next chunk while executing current) $\rightarrow$ effective 30ms latency. Total: $50\times$ speedup.

## Formal definition

The systematic process of collecting, filtering, balancing, and versioning demonstration datasets for training robot policies. Quality matters more than quantity: a policy trained on $N$ high-quality demonstrations often outperforms one trained on $10N$ noisy ones. Key considerations: state-action coverage $\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{D}) = \text{vol}(\text{support}(\mathcal{D}))$ over the relevant task manifold, data balance across modes/conditions, outlier removal, and consistent labeling. For multi-task datasets, maintain per-task statistics and ensure no task is severely underrepresented ($N_{\text{task}_i} \geq N_{\min}$ for all $i$).

## Formal definition

Rigorous assessment of robot policy performance. Primary metric: success rate $\hat{p} = \frac{\text{successes}}{N_{\text{trials}}}$ with Wilson confidence interval: $$\hat{p} \pm z_{\alpha/2} \sqrt{\frac{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})}{N_{\text{trials}}}}$$ Includes: failure mode analysis, sim-to-real transfer gap $\Delta = p_{\text{sim}} - p_{\text{real}}$, generalization tests (novel objects, positions), robustness tests (perturbations), and statistical significance across seeds ($n \geq 3$).

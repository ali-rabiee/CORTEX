## Failure modes

ODE solver instability: the learned vector field $v_\theta$ may have regions where the flow is poorly defined (large Lipschitz constant), causing numerical issues. Symptoms: occasional action jumps or NaN outputs. Fix: use adaptive ODE solvers (Dopri5), train with flow regularization $\mathcal{L}_{\text{reg}} = \mathbb{E}[\|\nabla_\mathbf{x} v_\theta\|^2]$, clip extreme vector field values during inference.

## Interview-ready answer

Flow matching is emerging as a cleaner alternative to diffusion for robot action generation. The advantage: simpler training objective (regress to the conditional vector field $v_\theta(\mathbf{x}_t, t) \approx \mathbf{x}_1 - \mathbf{x}_0$), faster inference (5-10 ODE steps vs 20-100 diffusion steps), and no noise schedule to tune. $\pi_0$ from Physical Intelligence shows this works at scale for multi-task robot control. Compared to diffusion policies, flow matching offers similar multimodality with simpler training and faster inference. I expect flow matching to become the default generative approach for robot control.

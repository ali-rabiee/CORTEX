## Formal definition

Flow matching learns a continuous-time vector field $v_\theta(\mathbf{x}, t)$ that transports samples from a simple distribution $p_0 = \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{0}, \mathbf{I})$ to the target distribution $p_1$ (actions). The trajectory follows the ODE: $\frac{d\mathbf{x}}{dt} = v_\theta(\mathbf{x}, t)$ for $t \in [0, 1]$. Conditional flow matching (CFM) enables efficient training by regressing to the conditional vector field: $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{CFM}} = \mathbb{E}_{t, q(\mathbf{x}_1)} \left[ \| v_\theta(\mathbf{x}_t, t) - (\mathbf{x}_1 - \mathbf{x}_0) \|^2 \right]$$ where $\mathbf{x}_t = (1-t)\mathbf{x}_0 + t\mathbf{x}_1$ is the linear interpolation path. Simpler training than diffusion (no noise schedule), faster inference (fewer ODE solver steps).

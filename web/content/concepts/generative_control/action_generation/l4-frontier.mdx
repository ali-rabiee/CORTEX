## Generate the trajectory, not the action

<PaperCard refKey="janner2022diffuser" />

The reframing that starts this line: make the *trajectory* the thing the model
generates. Planning then becomes sampling, a goal becomes conditioning, and a
constraint becomes a region the density avoids.

The property worth naming is that temporal coherence stops being something you
have to enforce. When the sampled object is the whole sequence, the
$M^{1-H}$ incoherence of per-step independence simply cannot arise — the model
never makes $H$ separate decisions.

<PaperCard refKey="ajay2023decisiondiffuser" />

Pushes it further: condition on the desired return and drop the value function.
This trades bootstrapping — with its divergence and overestimation problems — for
supervised conditional generation, and inherits the corresponding limit, since
conditioning on a return better than anything in the data is extrapolation.

<Steps>
<Step title="Diversity is the thing being preserved">
Pearce et al. make the point in a setting where it is easy to see: human
behaviour is varied, and a regression head produces a single averaged way of
acting that reads as inhuman.
</Step>

<Step title="Goal conditioning fits naturally">
Reuss et al. train on play data with goals as conditioning, which is the same
move Diffuser makes for constraints applied to a policy.
</Step>

<Step title="And latency became the axis">
Their treatment of sampler choice and step count against quality is the practical
question this whole cluster ends on — the step budget for a policy is set by the
control rate.
</Step>
</Steps>

<KeyIdea>
Read together, the claim is about the object rather than the machinery. Moving
the unit of generation from an action to a sequence is what delivers coherence,
conditioning and constraint handling; diffusion was the first convenient way to
do it, and flow-matching or autoregressive heads over chunks obtain the same
property. That is also why this concept sits next to action chunking rather than
under it: chunking says predict $H$ steps, and this says the $H$ steps have to be
one object.
</KeyIdea>

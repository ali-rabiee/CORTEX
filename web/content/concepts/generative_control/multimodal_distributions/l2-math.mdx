## Formal definition

When multiple valid action sequences exist for the same observation (e.g., reaching left or right around an obstacle). A single Gaussian $\mathcal{N}(\mu, \Sigma)$ collapses modes by averaging. Solutions: (1) Gaussian mixture models: $p(a|o) = \sum_{k=1}^{K} \pi_k \mathcal{N}(a; \mu_k, \Sigma_k)$; (2) Conditional VAEs: $p(a|o) = \int p(a|z,o)\, p(z|o)\, dz$ with latent $z$ selecting the mode; (3) Diffusion/flow matching: implicit multi-modal density through iterative refinement from noise.

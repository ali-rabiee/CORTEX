## Intuition

Asked to go around a table, you can go left or right — both are correct. A single Gaussian averages these into 'go straight through the table.' Multi-modal methods represent both options and commit to one during execution. This is why diffusion policies work so well for manipulation — real tasks have many valid solutions.

## In practice

Block stacking: the gripper can approach from any direction. BC with MSE loss $\mathcal{L} = \|a - \hat{a}\|^2$ averages all approach angles $\rightarrow$ hovers above block without committing. Diffusion policy samples one valid approach direction each time $\rightarrow$ clean, committed trajectories with varying approach angles across rollouts.

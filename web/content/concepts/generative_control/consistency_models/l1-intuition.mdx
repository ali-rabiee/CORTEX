## Intuition

Diffusion models are like taking 100 small steps from noise to a clean action — accurate but slow. A consistency model learns to take a single giant leap from any noise level directly to the final answer. Like training a student who, instead of solving every intermediate step of a math problem, learns to jump straight to the answer. The trade-off: slightly less precise, but 10-100x faster.

## In practice

Consistency Policy (Prasad et al.): distill a pre-trained diffusion policy into a consistency model for real-time robot control. Original diffusion policy: 10 DDIM steps at 50ms = 500ms per action chunk. Consistency model: 1-2 steps at 50ms = 50-100ms. On Push-T and robotic manipulation benchmarks: achieves $> 95\%$ of the diffusion policy's success rate with $5\text{-}10\times$ faster inference. Enables 20Hz+ closed-loop control on hardware with a single forward pass.

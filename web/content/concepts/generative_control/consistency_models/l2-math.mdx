## Formal definition

Consistency models learn a function $f_\theta(\mathbf{x}_t, t)$ that maps any point along a diffusion trajectory directly to the clean sample $\mathbf{x}_0$, enforcing the self-consistency property: $f_\theta(\mathbf{x}_t, t) = f_\theta(\mathbf{x}_{t'}, t')$ for all $t, t'$ on the same trajectory. Can be trained via consistency distillation from a pre-trained diffusion model: $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{CD}} = \mathbb{E}_{t, \mathbf{x}_0} \left[ d\big(f_\theta(\mathbf{x}_{t_{n+1}}, t_{n+1}),\; f_{\theta^-}(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{t_n}, t_n)\big) \right]$$ where $\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{t_n}$ is one DDIM step from $\mathbf{x}_{t_{n+1}}$, $\theta^-$ is the EMA target, and $d$ is a distance metric (e.g., LPIPS or $L_2$). Also trainable from scratch via consistency training (CT) without a teacher model.

## Formal definition

Uses denoising diffusion probabilistic models (DDPMs) to generate robot action sequences. The forward process adds Gaussian noise over $T$ steps: $q(\mathbf{a}^t | \mathbf{a}^{t-1}) = \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{a}^t; \sqrt{1-\beta_t}\,\mathbf{a}^{t-1}, \beta_t \mathbf{I})$. The learned reverse process iteratively denoises: $p_\theta(\mathbf{a}^{t-1} | \mathbf{a}^t, \mathbf{o}) = \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{a}^{t-1}; \mu_\theta(\mathbf{a}^t, t, \mathbf{o}), \sigma_t^2 \mathbf{I})$, conditioned on observations $\mathbf{o}$. Training minimizes: $$\mathcal{L} = \mathbb{E}_{t, \mathbf{a}^0, \boldsymbol{\epsilon}} \left[ \| \boldsymbol{\epsilon} - \boldsymbol{\epsilon}_\theta(\mathbf{a}^t, t, \mathbf{o}) \|^2 \right]$$ Naturally handles multimodal action distributions — can represent multiple valid ways to accomplish a task.

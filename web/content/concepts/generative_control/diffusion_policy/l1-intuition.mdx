## Intuition

Imagine a sculptor starting with a rough block of marble (noise) and iteratively refining it into a statue (action sequence). Each denoising step adds detail. The key advantage over BC: if there are two valid ways to pick up a mug (left side or right side), diffusion can represent both modes instead of averaging them into an invalid middle path.

## In practice

Diffusion Policy for bimanual manipulation: 2-step observation history (images + proprioception) $\rightarrow$ U-Net-based diffusion model $\rightarrow$ predict 16-step action chunks. With DDIM sampling ($\eta = 0$, 10 denoising steps instead of 100), runs at 10Hz on a GPU. Outperforms BC by 20-40% on multi-modal manipulation tasks.

## Formal definition

Estimating the full 6-DOF pose (3D rotation $R \in SO(3)$ + 3D translation $\mathbf{t} \in \mathbb{R}^3$) of known objects from RGB or RGB-D images. The pose transforms a point from object frame to camera frame: $\mathbf{p}_{\text{cam}} = R\,\mathbf{p}_{\text{obj}} + \mathbf{t}$. Methods: template matching, keypoint detection + PnP, direct regression, render-and-compare. Rotation representations: quaternion $\mathbf{q} \in \mathbb{S}^3$, rotation matrix $R \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3}$, 6D continuous representation (Zhou et al.).

## Formal definition

Representations extracted from visual observations for downstream robotics tasks. Hierarchy of abstraction: (1) Raw pixels $I \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W \times 3}$ — full information but high-dimensional and lacks invariances; (2) Feature maps from pretrained CNNs — intermediate activations $\phi_l(I) \in \mathbb{R}^{h \times w \times c}$ at layer $l$ capturing increasingly abstract features (edges $\rightarrow$ textures $\rightarrow$ parts $\rightarrow$ objects); (3) Keypoints $\{(u_k, v_k)\}_{k=1}^{K}$ — sparse spatial anchors detected via learned detectors or classical methods (SIFT, ORB); (4) Dense embeddings from foundation models: DINO features for semantic correspondence, CLIP embeddings $\mathbf{z} = f_{\text{CLIP}}(I) \in \mathbb{R}^{d}$ for vision-language alignment; (5) Spatial action maps $\pi(a|I) \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W}$ — pixel-level policy outputs. Choice of representation determines sample efficiency, generalization, and sim-to-real transfer.

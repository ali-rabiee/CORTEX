## Formal definition

Holistic comprehension of 3D environments beyond per-object recognition: (1) Scene graphs — directed graphs $G = (V, E)$ where nodes $v_i$ represent objects with attributes (class, pose, size) and edges $e_{ij}$ encode spatial relationships (on, inside, next-to, supporting); (2) Spatial relationship reasoning — predicting pairwise relations $r(o_i, o_j) \in \{\text{on, in, near, left-of, supporting, ...}\}$; (3) Room layout estimation — recovering 3D room geometry (floor, walls, ceiling) from single images; (4) Semantic SLAM — simultaneous localization and mapping augmented with semantic labels, maintaining a map $M = \{(\mathbf{p}_i, l_i, \sigma_i)\}$ of 3D points with class labels and uncertainties; (5) 3D scene reconstruction — building complete 3D models via NeRF, 3D Gaussian Splatting, or volumetric fusion (TSDF).

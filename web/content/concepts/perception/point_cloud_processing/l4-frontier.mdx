## Invariance, solved and paid for

<PaperCard refKey="qi2017pointnet" />

The architecture is almost minimal: embed each point independently, take an
elementwise max, classify. That is enough to be a genuine set function, and the
paper proves both what it can represent and what determines its output.

The critical-set theorem is the half worth carrying. Measured on a trained model,
93.6 of 1024 points determined the answer, and removing everything else changed
nothing in 512 of 512 clouds. Robustness and blindness are one property.

## Putting the structure back

<PaperCard refKey="qi2017pointnetpp" />

PointNet jumps from per-point features to a global descriptor with nothing
between, so it has no analogue of the local-to-global hierarchy that makes
convolutional features effective.

<Steps>
<Step title="Sample centres, group neighbours, apply PointNet locally">
Each neighbourhood gets its own descriptor by the same construction.
</Step>

<Step title="Repeat on the resulting sparser set">
Which builds the hierarchy — local geometry composed into larger structure.
</Step>

<Step title="And density becomes a first-class problem">
Once neighbourhood radius is a parameter, the $1/Z^2$ variation matters
explicitly. The paper proposes multi-scale grouping to cope.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Learning the aggregator

<PaperCard refKey="zhao2021pointtransformer" />

Max is a fixed choice of how to combine neighbours. Attention makes it learned
while staying permutation invariant — a weighted sum with input-dependent
weights, symmetric provided the weights are.

<KeyIdea>
This also dissolves the critical-set bound rather than merely improving accuracy.
A max lets one point per dimension through; a weighted sum lets every neighbour
contribute, so features that live in the diffuse 91% remain reachable. That is
the principled reason attention helps here, and it is a better answer than
"transformers work well" — the bound was a property of the aggregator, and
changing the aggregator removes it.
</KeyIdea>

## Into policies

<PaperCard refKey="ze2024dp3" />

The robotics argument is about what the model does not have to learn. Geometry is
measured directly by a depth sensor and expensive to infer from pixels, and
demonstration count — not compute — is the binding constraint. Supplying the 3D
structure explicitly is the same kind of move as delta actions or a canonical
frame: remove a variation the model would otherwise have to acquire from data.

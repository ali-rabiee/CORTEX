## Intuition

When you look at a mug, you instantly know: the handle affords grasping, the rim affords pouring from, the body affords pushing. Affordance prediction gives robots this same intuition — instead of recognizing 'this is a mug,' the robot sees 'this region is graspable, that region is pushable.' It shifts from object-centric to interaction-centric perception, which is what manipulation actually needs.

## In practice

Where2Act / AffCorrs pipeline: RGB-D observation $\rightarrow$ pre-trained visual encoder (DINOv2) $\rightarrow$ per-pixel feature extraction $\rightarrow$ affordance head predicts per-pixel action success probability for each primitive (push, pull, grasp, rotate). At test time: select pixel with highest $p_{\text{success}}$ for the desired primitive, extract local geometry to parameterize the action (approach direction from surface normal, gripper width from local curvature). Enables zero-shot transfer to novel objects via geometric/semantic similarity in feature space.

## Formal definition

Intrinsic calibration: focal lengths $(f_x, f_y)$, principal point $(c_x, c_y)$, distortion coefficients $(k_1, k_2, p_1, p_2, k_3)$. The projection model maps 3D to pixel: $$\begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} f_x & 0 & c_x \\ 0 & f_y & c_y \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} X/Z \\ Y/Z \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$$ Extrinsic calibration: transformation $T \in SE(3)$ between camera frame and world/robot frame. Hand-eye calibration: $AX = XB$ problem to find the fixed transform $X$ between camera and robot end-effector (eye-in-hand) or camera and robot base (eye-to-hand).

## Intuition

Raw pixels contain too much irrelevant information (shadows, textures, lighting) and not enough task-relevant structure (object pose, contact state). Representation learning compresses images into a feature space where 'similar' means 'similar for the task' — two images of a cup in different lighting should have similar features if the cup is in the same pose.

## In practice

R3M: pre-trained on Ego4D human video data with time-contrastive + language-alignment objectives. Fine-tune a manipulation policy with R3M features instead of raw pixels: $\sim 10\times$ fewer demonstrations needed. DINOv2 features also work well as frozen encoders for manipulation with just a linear probe $\hat{a} = W \phi(I) + b$.

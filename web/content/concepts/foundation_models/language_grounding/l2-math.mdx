## Formal definition

Connecting natural language to physical actions, objects, and spatial concepts in the real world. Formalized as learning a grounding function $g: \mathcal{L} \times \mathcal{O} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ that maps a language expression $l$ and observation $o$ to a grounded entity $e$ (object, region, or action). Includes: referring expression comprehension ('pick up the red cup near the edge'), affordance prediction ('what can I do with this?'), and instruction following ('stack the blocks by color').

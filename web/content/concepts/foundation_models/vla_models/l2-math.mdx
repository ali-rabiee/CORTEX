## Formal definition

VLAs are foundation models that map visual observations $o_t$ and language instructions $l$ to robot actions $a_t$: $\pi_\theta(a_t | o_t, l)$. They extend VLMs with action prediction heads, trained on large-scale robot demonstration datasets. Architecture: vision encoder (ViT) $\rightarrow$ projection $\rightarrow$ language model backbone $\rightarrow$ action head. Actions often tokenized into discrete bins or predicted as continuous vectors via a diffusion/flow head. Examples: RT-2 ($\pi(a_t | I_t, l) = \text{PaLM-E}(\text{tokenize}(a_t) | I_t, l)$), Octo, OpenVLA.

## Formal definition

Mechanisms for embodied agents to maintain, retrieve, and reason over information across time horizons longer than the model's context window. Types: (1) Working memory: the transformer's context window, holding the current observation and recent history — limited to $L$ tokens ($L \in [4K, 128K]$). (2) Episodic memory: a retrieval-augmented store of past experiences $\mathcal{M} = \{(o_i, a_i, r_i, t_i)\}$, indexed by embedding similarity: $\text{retrieve}(o_t) = \arg\max_{m \in \mathcal{M}} \cos(\phi(o_t), \phi(m))$. (3) Semantic memory: structured knowledge (scene graphs, object databases) updated incrementally. Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) for embodied agents: encode current observation $\rightarrow$ retrieve relevant past experiences $\rightarrow$ inject into context $\rightarrow$ condition action generation.

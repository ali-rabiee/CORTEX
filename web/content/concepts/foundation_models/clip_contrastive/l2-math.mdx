## Formal definition

CLIP (Contrastive Language-Image Pre-training) learns a joint embedding space for images and text by training on 400M image-text pairs from the internet. Architecture: image encoder $f_I$ (ViT or ResNet) and text encoder $f_T$ (transformer) produce embeddings $z_I = f_I(I) / \|f_I(I)\|$ and $z_T = f_T(T) / \|f_T(T)\|$. Trained with symmetric InfoNCE (contrastive) loss over a batch of $N$ pairs: $$\mathcal{L} = -\frac{1}{2N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \left[ \log \frac{\exp(z_{I_i}^\top z_{T_i} / \tau)}{\sum_{j=1}^{N} \exp(z_{I_i}^\top z_{T_j} / \tau)} + \log \frac{\exp(z_{T_i}^\top z_{I_i} / \tau)}{\sum_{j=1}^{N} \exp(z_{T_i}^\top z_{I_j} / \tau)} \right]$$ where $\tau$ is a learned temperature. Enables zero-shot classification: compute similarity between image embedding and embeddings of class-name text prompts, select highest similarity.

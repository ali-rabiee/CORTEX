## Intuition

CLIP learns to place images and their matching text descriptions close together in a shared vector space, while pushing non-matching pairs apart. After training, you can classify an image without any labeled examples — just compute which text description ('a photo of a dog,' 'a photo of a cat') is closest to the image in embedding space. For robotics, this means a robot can understand novel objects by their text descriptions without ever having been trained on them specifically.

## In practice

CLIP for robot reward shaping: given a language goal 'place the red block on the blue block,' compute CLIP similarity $r_t = \cos(f_I(o_t), f_T(\text{goal}))$ as a dense reward signal for RL. As the robot's observation $o_t$ becomes more aligned with the goal description, the reward increases. This replaces hand-crafted reward functions. SigLIP (improved CLIP variant) is used in PaLI-X and RT-2 as the vision encoder. For open-vocabulary object detection: CLIP embeddings enable robots to find objects described in natural language without retraining the detector (OWL-ViT, Grounding DINO).

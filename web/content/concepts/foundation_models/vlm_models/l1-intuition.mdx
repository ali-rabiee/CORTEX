## Intuition

A VLM can 'see' and 'talk' about what it sees. Show it a robot workspace photo and ask 'what objects are graspable?' and it can reason about the scene. It's the eyes and reasoning brain, but not the hands — it can plan but not act (that's what VLAs add).

## In practice

Using GPT-4V for robotic task planning: capture workspace image $\rightarrow$ prompt: 'Given this scene, plan the steps to sort objects by color. Output as numbered actions.' $\rightarrow$ parse structured output $\rightarrow$ execute with low-level controller. Works for long-horizon tasks where classical planning fails due to perception complexity.

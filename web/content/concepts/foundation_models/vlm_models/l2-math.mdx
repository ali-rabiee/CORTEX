## Formal definition

Models that jointly process images and text, enabling tasks like visual question answering, image captioning, and visual reasoning. Architecture: vision encoder $f_\text{vis}$ (ViT/CLIP) $\rightarrow$ projection layer $W_p$ $\rightarrow$ language model $f_\text{LM}$. The visual tokens $\mathbf{z} = W_p \cdot f_\text{vis}(I)$ are concatenated with text tokens and processed autoregressively. Examples: GPT-4V, Claude Vision, LLaVA, Gemini. Training: often a two-stage approach — (1) pre-train projection on image-caption pairs, (2) instruction-tune end-to-end.

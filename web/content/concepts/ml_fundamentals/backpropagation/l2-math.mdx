## Formal definition

Backpropagation computes the gradient $\nabla_\theta L$ of a scalar loss $L$ with respect to all parameters $\theta$ by recursive application of the chain rule on the computational graph. For a composition $L = f_n \circ f_{n-1} \circ \cdots \circ f_1(x)$, the gradient is: $$\frac{\partial L}{\partial \theta_i} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial f_n} \cdot \frac{\partial f_n}{\partial f_{n-1}} \cdots \frac{\partial f_{i+1}}{\partial f_i} \cdot \frac{\partial f_i}{\partial \theta_i}$$ This reverse-mode automatic differentiation has $O(n)$ cost for $n$ parameters, compared to $O(n^2)$ for forward-mode when computing full Jacobians.

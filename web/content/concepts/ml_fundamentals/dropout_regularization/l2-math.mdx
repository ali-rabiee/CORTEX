## Formal definition

Dropout randomly sets each neuron's activation to zero with probability $p$ during training, then scales by $1/(1-p)$ (inverted dropout) so that expected values remain consistent at inference. For a layer activation $h$, the dropout operation is: $$\tilde{h}_i = \frac{m_i}{1 - p} \cdot h_i, \quad m_i \sim \text{Bernoulli}(1 - p)$$ At inference, all neurons are active with no scaling (due to inverted dropout). Dropout can be interpreted as training an implicit ensemble of $2^n$ sub-networks that share parameters, or equivalently as approximate variational inference over the network weights.

## Intuition

Dropout forces each neuron to be useful on its own, preventing groups of neurons from co-adapting to memorize training examples. It is like training a sports team where random players sit out each practice — every player must learn all positions, making the team robust. The ensemble interpretation: each forward pass samples a different sub-network, and at test time you implicitly average their predictions. MC-Dropout (multiple stochastic forward passes at inference) provides approximate Bayesian uncertainty estimates.

## In practice

In a robotic grasping success predictor (MLP head after a frozen ViT encoder), dropout $p = 0.5$ in the two hidden layers (512-dim) reduces overfitting on a 10K-example dataset. Without dropout, train accuracy reaches 99% but validation saturates at 78%. With dropout, train accuracy is 92% and validation reaches 86%. For uncertainty estimation during deployment, MC-Dropout with 10 forward passes provides calibrated confidence scores — the robot abstains from grasps with high predictive variance.

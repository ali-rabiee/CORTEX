## Intuition

Attention is a soft dictionary lookup. The query asks 'what am I looking for?', keys advertise 'what do I contain?', and values hold 'what do I deliver?'. The dot product $QK^T$ measures relevance, softmax converts it to a probability distribution over keys, and the output is the expected value under that distribution. The $\sqrt{d_k}$ scaling keeps the softmax temperature reasonable — without it, high-dimensional dot products would produce near-one-hot distributions that kill gradient flow.

## In practice

In a robotic manipulation transformer policy, cross-attention between robot state queries ($Q$ from proprioception) and visual keys/values ($K, V$ from ViT features) enables the policy to selectively attend to task-relevant image regions. With $d_k = 64$ and 8 heads, each head learns different spatial attention patterns — one might focus on the gripper, another on the target object. Attention weights are directly interpretable: visualizing them reveals which image patches drive each action dimension.

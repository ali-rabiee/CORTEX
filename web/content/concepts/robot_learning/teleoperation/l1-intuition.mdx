## Intuition

Like a puppeteer controlling a marionette to record a choreography that the puppet will later perform on its own. The quality of the puppet's solo performance depends entirely on how well the puppeteer could control it during recording. A clunky interface produces jerky, unnatural demonstrations that are hard to learn from.

## In practice

ALOHA teleoperation for bimanual manipulation: two leader robot arms that the human physically moves, mechanically coupled to two follower arms that mirror the motion in real-time. Records joint positions at 50Hz. Advantages: intuitive (same kinematic structure), low latency (\<10ms), high-quality demonstrations. Used to collect 50 demonstrations per task for ACT training. Alternative: Meta Quest VR controllers mapped to robot end-effector via $\Delta \mathbf{x}_r = k \cdot \Delta \mathbf{x}_{\text{VR}}$ with scaling factor $k$ for precision.

## Formal definition

Manipulation tasks involving sustained, complex contact interactions between the robot and objects — e.g., peg insertion, gear meshing, surface wiping, pivoting, in-hand reorientation. Contact dynamics are governed by complementarity constraints: $$0 \leq \lambda_n \perp \phi(q) \geq 0$$ where $\lambda_n$ is the normal contact force and $\phi(q)$ is the signed distance function. Friction follows Coulomb's law $\|f_t\| \leq \mu \lambda_n$ with the maximum dissipation principle. These interactions are discontinuous, making contact-rich tasks fundamentally harder to simulate, plan for, and learn.

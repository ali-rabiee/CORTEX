## Formal definition

Training policies $\pi_\theta(a|s)$ in simulation and deploying them on real robots. The reality gap — differences in physics, rendering, dynamics, and sensor noise between sim and real — causes policies to fail when transferred. Formally, we train in an MDP $\mathcal{M}_{\text{sim}} = (\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{A}, T_{\text{sim}}, R, \gamma)$ but deploy in $\mathcal{M}_{\text{real}} = (\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{A}, T_{\text{real}}, R, \gamma)$ where $T_{\text{sim}} \neq T_{\text{real}}$. Bridged via domain randomization, system identification, or fine-tuning.

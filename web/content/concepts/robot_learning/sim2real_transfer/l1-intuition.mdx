## Intuition

Like training a pilot only on a flight simulator, then putting them in a real cockpit. The simulator doesn't perfectly model turbulence, weather, or instrument lag. Domain randomization is like training in hundreds of slightly wrong simulators — the pilot learns to handle anything because no single sim was 'right.'

## In practice

Training a robot hand to rotate a Rubik's cube (OpenAI, 2019). Policy trained in Isaac Gym with massive domain randomization: friction $\mu \in [0.5\mu_0, 1.5\mu_0]$, mass $m \in [0.7m_0, 1.3m_0]$, joint damping $d \in [0.6d_0, 1.4d_0]$, visual texture randomization. $10^{11}$ sim steps, transferred zero-shot to real robot with ~70% success.

## Intuition

Instead of building separate perception (detect object) → planning (compute grasp) → control (execute motion) modules, you train one neural network that sees the camera image and outputs joint velocities. Like teaching someone to catch a ball by practice rather than by explaining physics equations.

## In practice

ResNet-18 encoder with spatial softmax $\text{softmax}(f_{ij}) \rightarrow (x_c, y_c)$ → 64-dim feature → MLP → 7-DOF joint velocities $\dot{q} \in \mathbb{R}^7$ for pick-and-place. Trained on 200 teleoperated demonstrations with random crops for augmentation. Key: spatial softmax preserves spatial structure (outputting expected 2D coordinates) better than global average pooling for manipulation tasks.

## Formal definition

End-to-end policies $\pi_\theta: \mathcal{O}_{\text{image}} \rightarrow \mathcal{A}$ that map raw visual observations (RGB/RGB-D images) directly to motor commands without hand-designed perception pipelines. Typically: CNN/ViT encoder $f_\phi: \mathbb{R}^{H \times W \times C} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^d$ → spatial features → policy head (MLP or transformer) $g_\psi: \mathbb{R}^d \rightarrow \mathcal{A}$ → actions.

## Formal definition

An iterative imitation learning algorithm that addresses covariate shift in behavior cloning by aggregating expert-corrected data from the learner's own state distribution. At iteration $i$: (1) execute current policy $\pi_i$ to collect states $\{s_t\}$, (2) query the expert $\pi^*$ for actions at those states, (3) aggregate $\mathcal{D}_{i+1} = \mathcal{D}_i \cup \{(s_t, \pi^*(s_t))\}$, (4) retrain $\pi_{i+1} = \arg\min_\pi \mathcal{L}(\pi, \mathcal{D}_{i+1})$. Provably reduces the error bound from $\mathcal{O}(T^2 \epsilon)$ (BC) to $\mathcal{O}(T \epsilon)$ where $\epsilon$ is the single-step error and $T$ is the horizon.

## Formal definition

Supervised learning on expert demonstrations: learn $\pi_\theta(a|s)$ directly from $(s_i, a_i)$ pairs collected by an expert $\pi^*$. The objective is: $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{BC}}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{(s,a) \sim \mathcal{D}_{\text{expert}}} \left[ \| \pi_\theta(s) - a \|^2 \right]$$ Simple and effective when demonstrations are plentiful and the state space is well-covered. Suffers from compounding errors due to covariate shift: training on $d_{\pi^*}(s)$ but executing under $d_{\pi_\theta}(s)$.

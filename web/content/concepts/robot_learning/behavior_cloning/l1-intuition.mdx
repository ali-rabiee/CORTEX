## Intuition

Like learning to drive by watching dashcam footage of a good driver and imitating their steering at each frame. Works great for situations the expert demonstrated. But the moment you drift slightly off the expert's trajectory — perhaps due to a minor prediction error — you enter states the expert never showed you, and errors compound: small drift → unfamiliar state → bigger error → more unfamiliar state.

## In practice

Learning a 6-DOF pick-and-place policy from 100 teleoperated demonstrations. With image observations and a ResNet encoder: BC achieves 60% success. Adding data augmentation (random crops, color jitter) brings it to 75%. DAgger with 20 additional corrective demonstrations reaches 90%. The compounding error grows as $\mathcal{O}(T^2)$ in the horizon length $T$ for standard BC.

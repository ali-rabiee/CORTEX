## Formal definition

Instead of predicting a single action $a_t$ per timestep, predict a sequence (chunk) of $K$ future actions $\{a_t, a_{t+1}, \ldots, a_{t+K-1}\}$ at once. The policy becomes $\pi_\theta: s_t \mapsto (a_t, a_{t+1}, \ldots, a_{t+K-1})$. Reduces the effective decision horizon from $T$ to $T/K$, mitigates compounding errors in BC (error scales as $\mathcal{O}((T/K)^2)$ instead of $\mathcal{O}(T^2)$), and produces smoother trajectories. Used in ACT (Action Chunking with Transformers) and diffusion policies.

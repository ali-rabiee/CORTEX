## Intuition

If you learn to cook in 1000 different kitchens — different stoves, different pans, different temperatures — you'll handle any new kitchen because you've seen enough variation. The real world is just kitchen #1001. The key: randomize enough that reality falls within your training distribution.

## In practice

Sim2real for robotic grasping: randomize object mass $m \in [0.01, 2.0]$ kg, friction $\mu \in [0.3, 1.0]$, table height $h \pm 5$ cm, camera position $\pm 2$ cm, lighting direction, gripper finger friction. Train with PPO across 1024 parallel environments. The policy learns invariance to all these factors.

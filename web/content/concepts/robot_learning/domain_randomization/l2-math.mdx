## Formal definition

Training with randomized simulation parameters $\xi \sim \mathcal{U}(\Xi)$ so the policy treats the real world as just another random variation. The objective becomes: $$\pi^* = \arg\max_\pi \mathbb{E}_{\xi \sim p(\xi)} \left[ \mathbb{E}_{\tau \sim \pi, T_\xi} \left[ \sum_{t=0}^H \gamma^t R(s_t, a_t) \right] \right]$$ Parameters randomized include: mass $m$, friction $\mu$, damping $d$, visual textures, lighting, camera pose, observation noise $\epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$, action delays $\Delta t$.
